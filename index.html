Approach

How the public layer works

Private products stay private until they can be extracted into clean standalone packages, templates, or developer-facing tooling. The public side is curated on purpose: fewer repos, clearer positioning, better reuse.

Principles

Operating rules

Private first

Business logic and live product internals stay private until they can be extracted cleanly.

Reusable by default

Public releases focus on helpers, templates, and infrastructure that can stand on their own.

Operational realism

Most public tooling comes from systems that are already being run on a real VPS and product stack.
